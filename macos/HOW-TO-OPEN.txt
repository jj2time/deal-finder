Deal Finder for Mac

1. Drag "Deal Finder.app" into your Applications folder.
2. Double-click it. The first time, macOS will say it can't check the app for malicious software,
   because it isn't from the App Store. Click Done (or OK), then:
   open System Settings > Privacy & Security, scroll down, and click "Open Anyway" next to Deal Finder.
   Double-click the app again and click "Open". You only do this once.
3. The first launch sets itself up (a few minutes, needs internet). Your browser then opens the dashboard.
4. Click Settings, add your Apify token and your Rightmove/Zoopla searches, save, then press Scan now.

Deal Finder keeps running in the background so it can scan daily. Open the app any time to get back to the
dashboard, or click Stop on the dashboard to turn it off.

Your settings and deals are saved in: your home folder > Library > Application Support > Deal Finder
